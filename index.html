03 / The global commitment

What does Goal 10 call for?

The United Nations calls for reducing inequality within and among countries. These target areas turn that commitment into practical priorities for governments and institutions.

10.1

Income growth

Improve income growth for the bottom 40 per cent of the population so it outpaces the national average.

10.2

Inclusion for all

Promote social, economic, and political inclusion regardless of age, sex, disability, race, ethnicity, origin, religion, or status.

10.3

Equal opportunity

Reduce unequal outcomes and challenge discriminatory laws, policies, and practices.

10.4

Fair policies

Use wage, fiscal, and social protection policies to move societies towards greater equality.

10.5

Fairer finance

Strengthen the regulation and monitoring of global financial markets and institutions.

10.6

Representation

Give developing countries a stronger voice in international economic and financial decision-making.

10.7

Safe migration

Support safe, orderly, regular, and responsible migration and mobility.

10.a–10.b

Global cooperation

Support developing countries through fair trade arrangements and development finance.

10.c

Affordable remittances

Lower the cost of sending money across borders so more reaches families and communities.

04 / Turn awareness into action

Equality is built by what we do.

Change happens through everyday choices and larger public decisions. These are practical starting points—not substitutes for the policies and systems that must also change.

01

Notice the barriers

Pay attention to who is missing, excluded, underpaid, or unable to access a service. Listen before assuming what people need.

02

Make room for every voice

Share opportunities, challenge stereotypes, credit people's work, and include affected communities in decisions.

03

Support fair systems

Learn about equal access, decent work, social protection, accessibility, anti-discrimination, and safe migration—and support policies that advance them.

05 / About Sustainable Development Goal 10

Different lives.
Equal dignity.

SDG 10 is the United Nations goal to reduce inequality within and among countries. It calls for more than equal treatment: people must have a fair chance to participate in society and shape the decisions that affect their lives.

Inequality can be linked to income, age, sex, disability, race, ethnicity, origin, religion, or other circumstances. Goal 10 responds through inclusion, equal opportunity, fairer policies, stronger representation, and safe migration.

This project uses photography as a starting point for reflection. The images are visual metaphors; the stories and target summaries explain the issues they invite us to consider.
